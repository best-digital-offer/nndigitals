Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This policy describes how N&N Digitals handles information in connection with its websites, software, browser extensions and digital products. A specific product may provide additional privacy notices where its features require them.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on the product or service you use, information may include account details such as your name and email address; information you submit to support; subscription and transaction references; and technical or usage information needed to operate, secure and improve a service. We aim to collect only information reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

2. How information is used

Information may be used to provide and maintain services, authenticate accounts, process purchases and subscriptions, deliver customer support, prevent misuse, communicate important service notices, and meet legal obligations. We do not use payment card details as our own payment database when checkout is handled by a payment provider.

3. Payments and service providers

Payments may be handled by third-party payment processors. When you make a purchase, the processor handles payment information under its own privacy terms. We may receive limited transaction details, such as a transaction identifier, plan, status and billing-related information, to manage access and customer support. We may also use hosting, authentication, database, email-delivery and analytics providers where needed to operate a service.

4. Browser extensions and device information

Some products may require browser permissions or process information locally or through a service to perform the features you request. The permissions and data handling relevant to a product should be explained in that product's listing and privacy disclosures. You can review and manage extension permissions through your browser settings.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information with service providers acting on our behalf, when you direct us to do so, to protect rights and security, or when required by law. Service providers are expected to use information only for their assigned purposes.

6. Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described here, including account operation, support, dispute resolution, security and legal recordkeeping. We use reasonable safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of certain personal information, or object to or restrict some processing. You may also stop using a product or uninstall a browser extension. To make a privacy request, contact support@nndigitals.com.

8. Children

Our services are not intended for children where parental consent is required. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

9. International processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, appropriate safeguards will be used for international transfers.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as our services or legal obligations change. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date. Questions can be sent to support@nndigitals.com.
