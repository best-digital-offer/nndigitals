About the studio

Practical digital tools, thoughtfully made.

N&N Digitals is an independent digital product studio focused on creating useful software, browser extensions and digital tools that help people complete everyday online tasks with less friction.

Our purpose

We look for specific problems worth solving and build focused products around them. Our aim is to make each product understandable, straightforward to use and clear about what it offers.

How we work

  • Focused: products are designed around clear tasks and practical use cases.
  • Transparent: we aim to explain product features, pricing, permissions and limitations before a customer makes a decision.
  • Continuously improving: products may receive updates, fixes and feature improvements over time.

Our products

N&N Digitals may offer browser extensions, web-based utilities, software subscriptions and other digital products. Each product page or checkout should describe the specific product, included features, applicable price and any important limitations.
