How to Take a Full Page Screenshot in Chrome

By N&N Digitals · full page screenshot in Chrome

A regular screenshot captures only what is visible in the browser window. Long articles, product pages, dashboards and documentation often extend far below that area. A full-page screenshot captures the larger webpage in one image, making it easier to review, annotate and share a complete visual reference.

Build a practical workflow

Open the webpage you want to preserve and wait for its content to finish loading. Launch TabSnap, choose the relevant tab and start a full-page capture. If the page loads images or sections as you scroll, allow those elements to appear before capturing. Close pop-ups and menus that should not be part of the final image.

Keep your information organized

Full-page screenshots are useful for website QA, design feedback, research notes, saving online receipts for personal records and documenting a page layout. They are especially helpful when a reviewer needs to see the relationship between a page header, content sections and footer without stitching together several separate screenshots.

Important considerations

For best results, check the final image from top to bottom. Sticky navigation, lazy-loaded content, animations and pages that update dynamically can affect a capture. If an important section is missing, reload the page, let it settle and capture again. Always remove private account details before sharing a screenshot.

Choose the right approach

Choose PNG when you need an image for annotation or a slide. Choose PDF when you want a portable document that is easy to share or store with project notes. A screenshot is static: links, forms and interactive elements will not behave like the original webpage. Respect copyright, privacy and website terms when saving or distributing captures.

Explore the related tool

TabSnap is a Chrome extension for capturing full-page screenshots of all open tabs or selected tabs, with PNG and PDF export. Try it if you regularly need to capture long webpages without manually taking several screenshots.
