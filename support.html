Help and product assistance

We're here to help.

Need help with an N&N Digitals product, subscription, installation or account? Email our support team. To help us route your request correctly, please mention the product name clearly in the subject line and in your message.

Contact product support

support@nndigitals.com

When contacting us, start your subject with the product name. For example: TabSnap — Installation Help or SameWindow for ChatGPT — Billing Question.

Important: Always mention the product name.
We support multiple products. If the product name is missing, it may take longer for us to identify the correct team and assist you.

What to include in your email

  1. Product name: Tell us which N&N Digitals product you are using.
  2. Issue or question: Briefly explain what happened and what you expected.
  3. Helpful details: Include your browser, device, and any error message, if relevant.
  4. Order details: For payment or subscription questions, include the transaction or order reference if available.

Please do not email passwords, full payment-card numbers, security codes or other sensitive credentials. If possible, attach a screenshot of the issue with personal information hidden.

We aim to respond as soon as reasonably possible. Response times may vary depending on the nature and volume of enquiries.
