Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This policy applies to purchases of digital products and software services offered by N&N Digitals. Product-specific checkout terms may provide additional details. Nothing in this policy limits rights that cannot be excluded under applicable consumer law.

1. Subscription cancellation

Recurring subscriptions can be cancelled using the cancellation controls provided by the payment processor or by contacting support@nndigitals.com. Cancellation prevents future renewals. Unless otherwise stated at checkout or required by law, paid access remains available until the end of the current billing period.

2. Refund requests

If you experience a billing error, duplicate charge, inability to access a paid product, or another purchase issue, contact us with your order or transaction reference and a description of the issue. We will review the request and respond. Where a refund is approved, it will generally be processed through the original payment method by the payment processor.

3. Digital products and one-time purchases

For one-time digital purchases, please contact us promptly if the product was not delivered, cannot be accessed, or materially differs from its description. Refund eligibility will be assessed under the product's checkout terms and applicable law. A lifetime license means access for the supported lifetime of the product or service as described at purchase; it does not mean perpetual availability regardless of legal, technical or business circumstances.

4. Non-refundable circumstances

To the extent permitted by applicable law, a change of mind after digital content or access has been supplied may not qualify for a refund where the relevant legal requirements for digital-content exceptions have been met and any required consent or acknowledgement was obtained at checkout. This does not affect mandatory statutory rights or remedies for faulty, misdescribed or undelivered products.

5. Payment disputes

Please contact us first if you do not recognize a charge or believe you were billed incorrectly. You may also contact your payment provider. We may request reasonable information to locate the transaction and investigate.

6. Contact

Email support@nndigitals.com and include the purchase email address, product name and transaction reference. Do not send full payment card numbers or security codes.
